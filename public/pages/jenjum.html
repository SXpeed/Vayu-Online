Jenjum Gadi

Jenjum Gadi is a Nagaland-born contemporary artist and designer whose practice bridges indigenous craft traditions with modern material language. Trained at the National Institute of Design, his sculptural vessels, woven objects and mixed-media panels have been exhibited across India, London and Milan.

For Vayu, Jenjum presents an exclusive capsule — a series of collectible objects that echo the textile geometry of Naga weaves while embracing book-form brass, hand-turned teak and unfired earth finishes. Each piece is numbered and made in his Delhi studio.
