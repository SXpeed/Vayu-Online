Rooted in Culture.

Vayu is a cultural art and design space from New Delhi, celebrating Indian craftsmanship in all its contemporary glory. We collaborate with independent designers and master artisans across the subcontinent.

Our philosophy is simple: surround yourself with objects that have a story. From hand-carved teakwood furniture to meticulously woven textiles, every piece in our curation is chosen for its integrity, beauty, and timelessness.
