THE ARTISANS

The Makers

Honouring master artisans who carve, lathe and weave by living tradition.

At Vayu, every object begins with a pair of hands. We work with independent designers and master artisans across India — from the back-strap looms of Nagaland to the brass foundries of Moradabad, the stone carvers of Rajasthan and the potters of Pondicherry. Each maker brings decades, sometimes generations, of craft knowledge to the contemporary pieces you find here.

These are not anonymous hands. They are the architects of beauty, the quiet custodians of living tradition. Meet a few of the people behind the collection.

MEET THE ARTISANS

ARTIST IN RESIDENCE

Jenjum Gadi

New Delhi, Delhi

Nagaland-born contemporary artist and designer trained at the National Institute of Design. His sculptural vessels and woven objects bridge indigenous craft traditions with modern material language.

MASTER WEAVER

Lakshmi Devi

Chirala, Andhra Pradesh

A third-generation handloom weaver working with fine cotton and silk. Lakshmi's textiles carry the rythym of coastal Andhra's weaving communities, where each sari is a meditation in thread.

BRASS FOUNDER

Imtiaz Ahmad

Moradabad, Uttar Pradesh

Leading a small foundry that has practised the lost-wax casting technique for over four decades. Imtiaz's vessels celebrate the quiet warmth of hand-finished brass.

FURNITURE MAKER

Ramesh Suthar

Jodhpur, Rajasthan

A master woodworker specialising in reclaimed teak and acacia. Ramesh carves each joint by hand, letting the grain and imperfection of the wood guide the form.

CERAMICIST

Savita Kumar

Pondicherry

Trained at Golden Bridge Pottery, Savita works in stoneware and porcelain. Her pieces explore the tension between the wheel-thrown form and the spontaneous gesture of glaze.

STONE CARVER

Mohan Lal

Makrana, Rajasthan

From the famous marble town of Makrana, Mohan works with the same stone that built the Taj Mahal. His sculptural objects celebrate marble in its purest, most quiet form.

OUR PROMISE

01

Fair Craft

We pay fair wages above the industry standard and build long-term, direct relationships with every maker we work with.

02

Living Heritage

Each piece supports a craft tradition that has been practised for generations — keeping valuable knowledge alive in the modern world.

03

Natural Materials

We champion teak, brass, marble, clay and hand-spun textile — materials that age gracefully and return to the earth.
